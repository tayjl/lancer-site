# Pages

## Home, pilot lists and lore

- **Home** `/` (`src/views/HomeView.vue`, and where you land after logging in): extremely similar to the login screen (the developer's request, 2026-10-29). The same boot scrawl (`BootSequence.vue`: intro, h0r_os art, connection log, chat), fitted under the top bar, with buttons to every tab where the login screen has `[ uplink with discord ]` (GM and owner buttons for them). Options never wrap inside their brackets. In a narrow column (under 28rem, the art's breakpoint) each group is an even two-column grid, every button filling its cell at `--tap` height with 15px text (the most that fits `[ gm: changes ]` at 320px); an odd last option is centred across both columns; wider screens keep flowing rows. Home buttons are not automatically focused when the log finishes, so returning home does not falsely highlight my pilot as selected; keyboard navigation still uses the normal focus styling. Any key or click skips the animation.
- `my pilot` `/pilot`: your own pilot ([Pilot page (dossier)](pilot-and-mech-pages.md#pilot-page-dossier)), and `/pilot/mechs/:mechId` your mechs
- `registry` `/registry`: pilot cards in three tabs, Approved, Pending and Memorial ([Registry](#registry))
- Pending list (`/registry?list=pending`, old `/pending` redirects): new pilots a player has **submitted** and a GM hasn't decided on (drafts aren't listed; oldest submission first), **only for players to look at and discuss**. GMs don't review from here (decided by the developer, 2026-10-29).
- Memorial list (`/registry?list=memorial`, old `/memorial` redirects): dead pilots
- `combat` `/combat` (`src/views/CombatView.vue`): a read-only combat guide. It teaches the campaign's own diceless, mapless ruleset, [docs/combat-handbook.md](combat-handbook.md) (the developer's source text), kept in `src/combatRules.ts` in the Site text style with every number kept. The page is a summary of your turn (`CombatTurn.vue`: your mech, its 8 key numbers, the turn steps and scene resources; a left column on desktop, on the first two tabs on a phone), a search box, and 8 tabs (`?tab=`, with `?mech=`): Your kit, Actions, Attacks, Checks, Statuses, Movement and range, Structure, Posting. Each tab is cards of rows (`CombatGroup.vue`). Rules rows open in place and show a one-line gist (`gist` in `combatRules.ts`, else the first sentence). Your kit rows (`src/combatLoadout.ts`) open the full-information dialog like the mech page. Actions are grouped by activation, with your kit's own actions under a "Your kit" label. Tags and weapon sizes show only what your gear uses ("Show all tags" restores them). Searching adds an All tab and counts on every tab. The handbook's section 9 is merged into the matching rules. Its dice row uses the site's rounding (1d6 = 4).
- `pithub` `/pithub` (hidden, 2026-11-21): lore's table `pithub_entries` in its own terminal view, `src/views/PithubView.vue` (redesigned 2026-11-23): one login-style column, a glitching `PITHUB://` title inside an ASCII banner and status block (rogue-AI flavour, pit/pithom wording only, no glitched words, all aria-hidden, clipped to any width), a framed `/pit/query` search, damavand station wording (the AI "pithom" is an entity, not a place), an optional picture per file (`pithub_entries.image`, path only: shown after the body in its own `.omnimgf` code box, hidden if it fails to load; the editor's Image panel uses `ImageUpload`, see [Images](pilot-and-mech-pages.md#images)), `.omnif` (lancer omni file) names, an `ls -la` file list (size, modified, name) in its own ASCII-framed window (`fileName()` / `fileSize()` in `src/lore.ts`) and a file opened in the same page with `[ back ]`, `[ copy link ]` and (GM) `[ edit file ]`. No kinds, summary, author or related entries. The list types in once per session and any key or click skips it, a file's header types in each time it opens, reduced motion shows everything at once. `LoreEditView` serves both sections and hides Kind, Summary and Related entries when `section.plain`; the stores come from `LORE_SECTIONS` (one `loreStore` per table). No in-body links. Entry point: the home page's big art, once the boot finishes. Not secret: the route ships in the bundle, drafts stay behind RLS.
- `lore` `/lore` (`src/views/LoreView.vue`, `LoreEditView.vue`, `src/components/LoreEntry.vue`, `src/lore.ts`; table `lore_entries`, migration `20261116210000_lore_entries.sql`, added 2026-11-16, mockup `docs/redesign/lore/`, Option A): short stories, places, factions, people and events the GMs write. **Routes** (all `meta.section` `lore`): `/lore` (list, newest entry in the pane), `/lore/:id` (`lore-entry`: that entry; these urls are shared on Discord) and `/lore/:id/edit` (`lore-edit`, GM only). `App.vue` keys the lore list and entry views as one page, so the list keeps its place and search while you read. **List + pane** copies the archives' pattern (22rem sticky list, pane beside it over 56rem, arrow keys move and choose): a labelled search (every word must appear in the title, summary or body text, `loreMatches()`; `Clear` and Escape), neutral toggle chips per kind (symbol, plural name, count; none chosen = all), a lock note "Only GMs can see drafts." for GMs, then a box per kind (Stories, Places, Factions, People, Events) of real links, newest first: title, then "12 Nov 2026 · 3 min read" (reading time = body words / 200 rounded up, at least 1); drafts show `.badge.draft` and "Edited <date>". Rows are `RouterLink`s (replacing on desktop). Phones: the list is a page and an entry is a page of its own with a back link "Lore". The pane (`LoreEntry.vue`) has crumbs, the kind chip, title, summary, "By <name> · date · read time", `Copy link` (full url, "Copied" for a moment), `Edit entry` (GMs), the body (`RichText.vue`, 68ch, line-height 1.7) and "Related entries" (only ids that exist and the user can see). Empty states: "No lore yet.", "Nothing matches that search.", "This entry isn't available." Nothing is loaded into the page through raw HTML. `src/lore.ts` holds the shared entries list and runs every write through one queue (a fetch waits for pending saves). **Editor:** a status bar (`.badge.draft` Draft / `.badge.approved` Published, the save status, `View entry`, `Publish` or `Unpublish` after an "are you sure?"; sticky except on phones), Title (saved on leaving; empty refused), Kind (real radio inputs, saved on change), Summary (300 characters), Body (`SheetField` + `RichTextEditor` with `links`, autosaving), Related entries (removable chips, `Link entry` picker through `ItemPicker.vue`) and a Danger zone with `Delete entry`. New entries are created as drafts titled "Untitled entry". **Links between entries:** the editor's `links` prop adds a fourth toolbar group (`Link to entry`; disabled with "Select some text first"; on a link it removes it) using TipTap's Link mark from StarterKit (`editorExtensions(true)` in `src/richText.ts`: `openOnClick`, `autolink` and `linkOnPaste` all off; pilot-sheet editors have no link mark). `sanitiseDoc()` keeps a link only when its href is exactly `/lore/<uuid>` (strict regex) and drops every other attribute; any other link (javascript:, outside sites, other paths) is removed and its text kept (tested in `src/richText.test.ts`). `RichText.vue` draws a kept link as a `RouterLink` with the global `.lore-link` class (`style.css`: lilac and always underlined, 1px at about 50% lilac, full on hover and focus; text underlined with the editor's U stays plain). Tested in `src/lore.test.ts`.
- GMs and owners: `gm: pilots` `/gm/pilots` (review cards, then the searchable pilot records) and `gm: changes` `/gm/changes` (see [Approval](approval-and-security.md#approval))
- Owners: `gm: content` `/gm/content` ([LCP import](game-content.md#lcp-import))
- `/gm` redirects to `gm: pilots`. Route `meta.gm` / `meta.owner` mark GM and owner pages; pages check `isGm` / `isOwner` (`src/auth.ts`) and say "access denied" otherwise, in a `.notice.warn`. The database enforces the real rules.

## Registry

- **registry** (`src/views/RegistryView.vue`) is a centred grid of pilot ID cards (scoped `.id-grid`: one to a row on phones, two or three on desktop), with the count of pilots in the header. **Three lists in one page** (2026-11-21): underline tabs (`SheetTabs`) Approved (default), Pending (submitted, waiting on a GM) and Memorial (dead), kept in the url as `?list=pending` / `?list=memorial` and switched with `router.replace`. `/pending` and `/memorial` redirect to them. `pilotList()` in `src/router.ts` gives a record's breadcrumb its list's link.
  - **Search** (2026-11-15): a box above the cards filters the loaded cards by callsign, pilot name or player name (2026-11-21: no longer mech names, which the card doesn't show); every typed word must match (`pilotMatches()` in `src/pilots.ts`, tested in `src/pilots.test.ts`). It shows "x of y pilots" (dim, mono numbers) while filtering, and is hidden when the list is empty. A failed load shows a `.notice.warn` with the error.
  - **The card** (2026-11-21): each pilot's Union ID card (`PilotIdCard.vue`, see [ID cards and link previews](pilots.md#id-cards-and-link-previews)), the whole card a link to the record (its name is the card's label); no panel or border around it (the developer, 2026-11-21); hover and focus give it a lilac glow that follows the cut corners (`drop-shadow`), plus the focus ring on focus. No `Download card` (no `extra` passed) and no silent save (no `savePath`) here. Under it, centred and dim, "Player: x" (or "Unknown player"). Dead pilots need nothing extra: the card's Status says DEAD. Each link has `content-visibility: auto` so off-screen cards aren't drawn. The same card and grid serve pending and memorial (route `meta.status` picks the pilots, `meta.empty` is the text when there are none). `PilotCard.vue` is now only the GM queues' card.
  - **Clicking a card** opens the pilot record (`/registry/:id`). For visual congruity (the developer's request, 2026-10-29) **it's the my pilot page itself, read-only** (`PilotManageView.vue` with a route `id`), so a record always looks exactly like your own sheet. Its mech cards open the mech page read-only too (`/registry/:id/mechs/:mechId`, `MechView.vue`). The back link goes to the list the pilot is in.
    - `useMyPilot(id)` in `src/myPilot.ts` loads that pilot instead of yours, never editable. The `characters` select embeds `character_sheets`, which RLS only gives to the pilot's own player, GMs and owners; for everyone else (the embed comes back empty) it calls `pilot_sheet()` for the sheet without the backstory and manna, plus level, so every section shows for every viewer. `canSeeBackstory` (renamed from `canSeePrivate`; also exported as `canSeeManna`) says whether the whole row came back: only then the Backstory panel shows the real backstory (and its toggle) and the header shows manna; otherwise only a public copy of the backstory, if there is one, and no manna (the page treats the balance as unlimited so the rule check can't raise a false overspend; every price tag and balance block is editable-only anyway). Editing is unchanged: only the pilot's player (pending pilot or draft change) and GMs in edit mode.
    - GMs get a `GM review` link (pending pilots only) to the review page, in the header. The decision itself is never on the record. They also get an `Edit as GM` button (not on dead pilots): **GM edit mode**, below in this file.
    - **GM edit mode** (`?edit=1`, kept with `router.replace`; the record, the review page and the mech pages; `useMyPilot(id, { gmEdit: () => route.query.edit === '1' })` in `src/myPilot.ts`). Turns on `editable` for GMs and owners on any living pilot (pending or approved), so the sheet and mech pages are the same components a player uses on their own pending pilot, and every save goes straight to the pilot's `characters` / `character_sheets` rows (never a draft change), through the same save queue. `gmEditing` is false for non-GMs (a hand-typed `?edit=1` does nothing) and for dead pilots. **It works on a GM's or owner's own pilot too** (2026-11-19), so they can give their own characters Exotic gear (the only place Exotic can be picked): on My pilot an approved pilot's header has an `Edit pilot` button that opens their own record in edit mode (`/registry/:id?edit=1`; a pending one is already editable); it saves straight to the pilot, never to a draft, and the usual "change waiting" warning applies if their own draft change is open. **Exotic content can be picked only here** ([Content editor forms](game-content.md#content-editor-forms), The Exotic Gear tag). Mech cards and the mech breadcrumb keep `edit=1`. `GmEditBar.vue` (a bar joined to the header's foot, on the pilot and mech pages): a `GM edit` badge, "Saves go straight to this pilot. Rules and prices are off.", the save status and `Done`, and (pilot page only) a rewards row under a hairline (`GmRewards.vue`); with a `.notice.warn` when the player has a change open (`waiting`, loaded for GMs in edit mode), linking to it once submitted. A direct edit blocks that change from being approved (the "changed since" check, see [Approval](approval-and-security.md#approval)), by design: review it first. Sheet saves keep `sheetUpdatedAt` current, so approving a pending pilot after editing it isn't refused. Level, manna and experience are in the bar's rewards row. **Rules and prices are off** (2026-11-21): pickers drop blocked reasons and prices (`useGmOverride()`), and everything a GM adds is a free **GM grant** (`character_sheets.gm_grants`, recorded by `useMyPilot`; `mannaSpent` skips them) shown with a `GM grant` badge (shield plus words; dashed rank pips on licences and talents). The rule check still lists problems, as a warning. A GM in edit mode can edit and delete any service record entry. Also in edit mode: a `GM only` section (reputation, commendations, reward history) and the danger zone.
    - `App.vue` keys the `RouterView` by path, so going from one record to another loads the new pilot.
  - **Real data:** `fetchPilotCards()` in `src/pilots.ts` calls the `pilot_cards(target_status)` database function (security definer: level and licence level come from `character_sheets`, which other players can't read; nothing private), through `fetchAll()` (ordered by the list's date then id). It returns the card's fields plus the dates and the player's name, filtered like the old query (pending = submitted only, oldest first). `PILOT_FIELDS` and `PublicPilot` stay for the GM roster and the record pages.

## GM pages

- **gm: pilots** (`src/views/GmPilotsView.vue`, titled "GM pilots": each section a `.panel` with a left-aligned `.panel-head`):
  - **Waiting for review:** `ReviewQueue.vue`: a centred grid of `PilotCard`s ("Player: x", then "Waiting 3d 4h") with a plain count `.badge` beside the heading, oldest submission first (waiting time counts from `submitted_at`), linking to GM review. Submitted pilots only. With none waiting, the `.empty` state. Drafts and sent-back pilots stay in the roster (see Pilot records below) until their player submits again.
  - **Pilot records:** a full-width, scrollable table of every pilot (including dead), searchable by callsign, pilot name or player. Keep records compact in consistent single rows; on narrow screens the table scrolls horizontally within its container. Show callsign, name, player, status, level, licence level, manna received, manna left, mech names and a link to the full sheet. **Only level, experience and total manna received are editable**, saved when leaving the field through `award` (no reason; the row re-reads the pilot after each save); an **Experience** column after Level holds the number, a slim `.meter-bar` (0–1000) under it and, at 1000, a neutral `Ready to level` `.badge` with a small `Level up` button (after an "are you sure?", `level_up`); callsign and name are read-only. No give/take controls: they duplicate editing manna. Balance is received minus calculated spending; spending shows as small dim text under it (and on hover). Status is a `.badge` variant (an unsubmitted pending pilot is `Draft`, `.draft`; "Sent back" uses `.rejected` and shows the GM's note on hover); numbers are mono and right-aligned; the header row sticks while the table scrolls; each row shows "Saving…" and "Saved" (dim) or "Not saved: …" (`--danger`), like every other save status; `Open sheet` is a link drawn as a small button. Invalid numbers are refused and failed numeric saves restore the saved value. These are the developer's updated preferences (2026-10-29).
- **GM review** (`/gm/pilots/:id`, the read-only pilot page; for a pending pilot, `src/components/GmReview.vue` in a "GM review" section): opened from gm: pilots (`Open sheet`: pending pilots here, living ones straight into GM edit mode, dead ones read-only) or a pending pilot's `GM review` link on its record.
  - While the pilot is pending, the "Decision" panel (a plain `.panel`, no lilac border): a "Note to the player" `label.field`, the rule check, then **Approve pilot** (`.primary`) and **Send back** (`.danger`, note required); "Not submitted yet." for a draft. Nothing else is on the review page: every other GM job is in GM edit mode.
- **GM edit mode does everything** (2026-11-21, `?edit=1`, see [Registry](#registry)): rules, prices and blocked reasons are off, and the "GM controls" block is gone.
  - **Rewards row** in `GmEditBar` (`GmRewards.vue`): level, manna received and experience plus an optional **Reason**, saved with `Save rewards` (`award`: only the changed fields are sent, `Not saved: Nothing has changed.` otherwise); at 1000 experience a `Ready to level` badge and a `Level up` button (`level_up`). Also on pending pilots (starting values).
  - **GM only section** (`#gm-only`, after Hangar): Reputation, Commendations and the **Reward history** (`RewardHistory.vue`, newest first, latest 20 with `Show all`; the only block shown on a dead pilot).
  - **Danger zone** (end of the file): `Mark pilot dead` (GMs on an approved pilot in edit mode) and owners' `Delete pilot` (edit mode, or a dead pilot): through `confirmAction`; delete checks the returned row (RLS turns a refused delete into zero rows) and goes to `/gm/pilots`.
- **Death** (in scope, the developer's choice): a player marks their own approved pilot dead from the "danger zone" panel at the end of my pilot's file, after an "are you sure?"; GMs can do it from the danger zone in GM edit mode. Both run `mark_dead()` (database), which can't be undone and withdraws any waiting change. The pilot moves to the memorial (read-only record, a dim "dead" badge), and the player gets the register form for a new pilot.

## Login boot

- **No visible divider lines** between the login screen's sections; spacing separates them.
- **Glitching** only on the login screen's ASCII art (a colour-split jolt every few seconds) and the HORUS greeting in the archives (the developer's request, 2026-10-31). It's the shared `.glitch` class and `glitch` keyframes in `style.css`. Page titles no longer glitch (clear over stylised), except Pithub's `PITHUB://` title in `PithubView.vue` (the developer asked for heavy styling, 2026-11-21). On the home page (not login) the art becomes a link to `/pithub` once the whole boot has played (`BootSequence` `link` prop), with no hover hint. Never on body text or anything people read. It switches off for people who prefer reduced motion.
- **The login page is a boot scrawl** (`src/components/BootSequence.vue`), laid out as a full-screen terminal:
  - **Rows:** the h0r_os ASCII art pinned at the top, the log in the middle, and the `[ uplink with discord ]` option centred at the bottom (`src/views/LoginView.vue`; `--tap` high in a narrow column).
  - **Order:**
    1. COMP/CON's HORUS start-up text prints in the log, then the art fills its (reserved) space at the top, then the connection log and MOTD print. Text lines come every 320ms (intro) and 260ms (connection log); art lines every 35ms.
    2. The Uplink button appears, already selected.
    3. A fake HORUS chat keeps arriving in the log under the MOTD, in slightly smaller text, one message every 1.1s.
  - **The log doesn't scroll.** It works like `tail`: its height snaps to a whole number of lines, new lines appear at the bottom, and old ones drop off the top. Every line is the same height (the chat's line height matches the log's), so no line is ever cut in half. A wrapped message that's partly off the top is hidden.
- **Where to edit it:** the text lives in `src/assets/boot/` (`intro.txt`, `art.txt`, `connect.txt`, `chat.txt`).
  - `chat.txt` is ours to edit freely. Lines are `username: message`, optionally starting with `[ MOD ]` (purple tag) or `[ ADMIN ]` (red tag). The `username:` is sky blue and the message white. Inside a message, `@mentions` are sky blue and `<SYSTEM MESSAGES>` in angle brackets are purple. Ban notices wrapped in `// ... //` are entirely red. (All in `src/chat.ts`.)
  - **Small phones** (a column under 28rem) show `art.txt` without the arrow banner at the bottom: everything from the first `>>>>` line on is dropped, worked out in code, so there's only one file to edit. Bigger screens show all 27 lines. The art's size on phones is limited by its 80-column width (about 7px on a 375px phone), so dropping lines frees vertical space rather than enlarging it.
  - An empty line in any of the text files shows as a blank line.
  - The phrases highlighted in rose inside the art are listed in `ART_PARTS` in `BootSequence.vue`. Update that list if those words change in `art.txt`.
- **Behaviour:** any key or a click skips to the end, and it's instant for reduced motion.
- **Layout:** one centred column, at most 47rem wide, which matches the art at its 16px maximum. The art scales to fill the column (container query units), so the text and art share edges, and the art shrinks on phones instead of wrapping.
  - On shorter screens the art also shrinks to leave room for the log, and the column narrows with it, but not below 34rem.
  - On very short screens (under 30rem tall, e.g. a phone held sideways) nothing is pinned and the page scrolls normally.
  - Text is slightly larger on wide screens.
  - It comes from COMP/CON (`src/features/main_menu/_components/startup_logs/horus.ts`, GPL-3.0, Massif Press) and is credited in the README. The `∙` character was swapped for `•`, because no bundled font has `∙` and it would break the art's alignment.
