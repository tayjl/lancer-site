# Item cards

Covers: Item cards, symbols and tag names.
See also: [pickers-and-info.md](pickers-and-info.md), [mech-loadout.md](mech-loadout.md).

- **Item cards** (the prototype's ItemCard; pilot and mech loadouts, bionics, licences, talents, core bonuses): `ItemCard.vue` is the one card: a header bar in its type's colour (`--type-*`: weapon grey, system green, frame/licence purple, pilot gear/bionic teal, talent brown, core bonus lime with dark text) with the type's symbol, the name (an `InfoButton`) and, at the right, system points, mod points or a rank (hidden when 0); then the body and a foot for its buttons. Integrated items get a light line under the header. `ItemDetails.vue` fills it for a game item (`itemCard()`, `itemFacts()` and `itemRules()` in `src/itemDetails.ts`, tested in `src/itemDetails.test.ts`): the facts line (`.sep`: licence rank or maker, mount and type: "IPS-N Blackbeard II · Main Melee"; none for pilot gear), its numbers as symbol + mono number + small word (`ItemStats.vue`; damage symbols in their `--dmg-*` colour), its actions and deployables always in full, each on the card's surface under a thin rule (COMP/CON's active effects only when they're the item's only rules: `cardRules()`; `ActionChip.vue` first, then the name and frequency, the action's own range and damage, its rules), its rules text, "Where these numbers come from" (modifiers applied by `equipment.ts`) and its tag chips. `BuildItem.vue` wraps it on mechs, with the profile/prototype choices at the top of the body; a weapon with Variable damage gets a Damage type select (`Choose…` until picked, a rule problem until then, stored in `weapon_options[key].damageType`; pilot weapons: [pilot-inventory.md](pilot-inventory.md)). **Description** (2026-11-20): each equipped item (pilot gear, mounted mech weapons, installed systems; not built-in items) can carry its player's description, max 140 characters (`ItemNote.vue`): an italic line at the top of the body, or a `Description` text field while editable, saved on change. It goes through approval like the rest of the sheet, and goes when its item is swapped or removed (`src/itemNotes.ts`); pilot gear's lives on the owned copy (`owned_gear[].notes`), so it follows the item when it's swapped. `ActionChip.vue` is the clipped chip for Quick, Full, Reaction, Protocol, Free, Overcharge, Quick Tech, Full Tech and Invade (`activationOf()`: `--act-*` colour and cci symbol; passive draws nothing). `SlotCard.vue` is one place in a loadout: its name as a small uppercase label above (none for systems), then the card, or the empty state with a sentence and a button. Cards sit in a `.slot-grid` (`src/style.css`: CSS columns at least 20rem wide, each filling from the top like masonry, so short cards leave no gaps; cards read down each column, 2026-11-19). `ItemPicker.vue` is the searchable picker dialog. Cards for pack content (not bionics) end with a small dim "Source: <pack name>" line (`source` prop, `.pack-source` in `style.css`); `sourceOf(id)` in `gameData.ts` looks the pack up from one shared read of `content_items`, refreshed after `invalidateCatalogues()`.
- **Symbols:** `src/components/Icon.vue` draws any SVG in `src/assets/icons/` in the text colour, by `name` (e.g. `<Icon name="lock" />`) or an imported url as `src` (logos, gameData's stat symbols). The folder holds LANCER's stat/range/damage symbols, COMP/CON's cci icons (actions, sizes, ranks, item types) and Lucide's (status, privacy, interface, HASE and personal stats), split from the prototype's sprite; credited in the README.
- **Tag names** come from lancer-data's tag list (`tagNames` in `src/gameData.ts`, loaded once), e.g. "Limited {VAL}" shows as "limited 3".
