# Dossier page

Covers: The pilot page as one scrolling file: cover, numbered sections, file index, identity, editing and shared pieces.
See also: [dossier-sections.md](dossier-sections.md), [pilot-inventory.md](pilot-inventory.md), [mech-page.md](mech-page.md).

- **my pilot is built** as a COMP/CON-style sheet in the centred page column (`src/views/PilotManageView.vue`; redesigned 2026-11-15):
  - **Breadcrumb** on records and GM review (`registry / WARDEN`, `pending /`, `memorial /` by status, or `gm: pilots /`); no big page title, the callsign is the title. The global `.crumbs` style (`style.css`) is shared with `PageHeader.vue`.
  - **Cover** (2026-11-20, replacing the old header; `PilotCover.vue`, mockup `docs/redesign/dossier/`): a banner strip (the picture, else a plain gradient from the pilot colour into `--void` with one soft glow, no stripes; the cover body is a flow-root so its faint grid starts under the banner, never across it), the portrait in pilot-colour corner brackets, a small mono classification line ("Pilot file · `pilotSerial(id)`") with the pilot's status `.badge` at its right end (before any GM links, alone in the corner without them; it wraps to its own line on phones, still visible), the callsign big with the name beside it on the same baseline (24px `--text-dim`, 20px / 18px on phones, wrapping under the callsign when there isn't room; both inline fields where editable; the name isn't repeated when there's no callsign), the motto (20px italic in pilot-colour quotation marks drawn by CSS, never stored; the editable textarea has no underline, only an outline on hover and focus), then a foot row with the medals (`CommendationBadges.vue`) and "Player: x" small and dim at its right end, under the actions (its own left-aligned line on phones), then the figures strip: Level, Licence level, Grit, HP and, only for the pilot's player and GMs, Manna left. While editing, the cover's `tools` slot holds the Portrait, Banner (`ImageUpload`, plus `Position banner`) and Colour (`PilotColourField`, `Reset colour` on the same line as the boxes) controls along its foot, three equal top-aligned columns under one rule (stacked on phones). **Banner positioning** (`Position banner`, and straight after a new banner saves): the strip comes in front of the portrait and is dragged (pointer events, so mouse and touch; `touch-action: none` while positioning) or nudged with the arrow keys when focused (Shift for bigger steps, Escape cancels); a panel over its foot holds a `Zoom` slider (1 to 3), `Save position` and `Cancel`. The position is `object-position` in percent plus `scale(zoom)` from the same point, so it covers the strip at any width. The faint grid and the corner brackets in `--pilot` are decoration. GMs get `GM review` / `GM controls` and `Edit pilot` at the right of the classification line, after the status badge.
  - **"What happens next" bar**: a `--void` band joined to the header's foot, on your own sheet and any dead pilot's: a sentence-case sentence (a badge only for a change, since the cover already shows the pilot's own status), the save status ("Saving…", "Saved", red "Not saved: …"; `useSaveStatus()` in `src/saveStatus.ts`, drawn by `SaveStatus.vue`, which the mech page, GM edit bar and lore editor use too), then `.small` buttons at the right (secondary first, at most one `.primary`): a new pilot as a draft ("Draft": "GMs see it once you submit it.", `Submit pilot`), submitted ("Pending": "Waiting on a GM.", read-only, `Edit pilot` takes it back to a draft, no confirm), sent back ("Sent back", the GM's note, editable, `Resubmit pilot`), approved (`Propose changes`), drafting ("Draft": `Withdraw change`, `Submit change`), a change a GM sent back ("Sent back", the GM's note, editable, `Withdraw change`, `Submit change`), submitted ("Submitted": `Edit change`, `Withdraw change`), dead (read-only). On phones the sentence takes a row and the buttons share the next. Long notes sit in a `.notice` under it with a `.notice-title` ("Note from the GM", with the quiet `Clear note` once approved; also for a sent-back change), then the rule check.
  - **One scrolling file, no tabs** (2026-11-20): numbered sections (numbers come from position among the visible ones), `#identity`, `#pilot-id`, `#service-record`, `#capabilities`, `#qualifications`, `#equipment`, `#hangar`, `#gm-controls` (GMs on the review route only), `#gallery` (`FileSection.vue`: mono number, title, a short pilot-colour dash on a hairline, extras at the right; on phones under 40rem the hairline hides and the extras sit at the right of the title, wrapping as one group; a section's own component teleports its buttons and count into `#<id>-extra`, as `PilotIdCard`, `ServiceRecord`, `MechHangar` and `PilotGallery` do). `FileIndex.vue`: a sticky list beside the file on wide screens, under 56rem one sticky line under the top bar showing the current section, which opens the same list (2026-11-20); an `IntersectionObserver` marks the section in view (`aria-current`), a click scrolls and `router.replace`s the `#hash`, so `/pilot#hangar` links and reloads land there (the mech page's back link is `#hangar`; the mech page uses the same index and sections under its own spec sheet, see [mech-page.md](mech-page.md)); a hash link keeps landing while content above it loads, until the reader scrolls. **Every section after Identity folds** (2026-11-20): the title is a button (`aria-expanded`, chevron before the number, lilac only on hover/focus), the body stays mounted with `v-show` so unsaved fields and open pickers survive, head extras stay visible; Identity is `fixed`. Open by default; each section's state is kept per viewer in localStorage (`src/fileSections.ts`, works without storage), and an index click or a `#section` link opens the section before scrolling. Every section shows for every viewer, except the gallery, which read-only viewers don't get when it's empty. Sections sit on the page rather than in `.panel`s; boxes stay for item cards, the ID card and the danger zone. GM controls (`GmReview`: the pending Decision form, or Rewards, reputation and the danger zone) is its own section on the review route (2026-11-20).
  - **01 Identity** (`IdentityGrid.vue` at full width: form-like cells for age, gender, sexuality, height, weight, eyes and hair, the last one widening so no cell is left empty; fields while editable), then Appearance, Personality and Backstory, one under another. The writing headings (`SheetField.vue`) are 16px, bold and tracked with a thin rule under the row, so they don't read as a log entry's label. **02 Pilot ID**: `PilotIdCard` alone, centred as a showcase (at most 36rem), its `Download card` in the section head. **03 Service record**: the timeline, full width. Your own approved pilot gets a "Danger zone" panel at the very end of the file (`Mark pilot dead`).
  - **Always editable, no edit buttons** (while pending): appearance, personality and backstory are open editors that save themselves 0.8s after you stop typing (`src/components/SheetField.vue`; an empty editor shows a quiet "write here..."). Callsign and name are inline inputs, the table normal inputs, all saved when you leave them. Traits are off the sheet for now (the `traits` column and the `Entry` type in `src/entries.ts` are kept; nothing shows them, and the list parser `parseEntries` was removed in the 2026-11-20 cleanup).
- **The pilot page is modular.** Each category (mechs, equipment, talents, licences, core bonuses, mech gear and so on) is a section with a `[ + ]` box to add an item.
  - For game items, `[ + ]` opens a searchable picker of pre-made items from the imported LCPs. This is how most players add things, and the sheet stores only the item's ID.
  - Mechs are added with a small form (name, manufacturer ID, appearance, image).
  - Paragraph fields (appearance, personality, traits, backstory) use a rich-text editor (TipTap, which stores formatting as structured data rather than raw HTML, so players can't inject code into each other's pages; `RichTextEditor.vue`, extensions from `editorExtensions()` in `src/richText.ts`; the TipTap-free cleaning and reading side, `sanitiseDoc()` and `docText()`, is `src/richTextSafe.ts`, so read-only pages (lore, change review) don't download the editor). Its toolbar offers bold, italic and underline; small, normal and large font sizes; and bullet lists and small headings. The toolbar is the field's header strip (`--raised`, one bordered box with the writing area under it, outlined lilac while anything inside has focus): three segmented groups, B / I / U, S / M / L and the Lucide list and heading symbols, parted by thin `--rule` lines, `--tap` sized, each with an `aria-label`, `title` and `aria-pressed` (pressed: lilac text on a faint tint). On phones it stays one row and scrolls sideways. Headings and lists are whole blocks, so with text selected part-way through a paragraph the heading and list buttons first split the paragraph at the selection's edges (`splitAroundSelection()` in `src/richText.ts`, one undo step, the space at each cut dropped, never inside a list item) and only the selected words change; with just a cursor they act on the whole block. **No text colours** (the developer, 2026-11-16): each colour has one meaning site-wide, so player-picked colours would read as links, warnings or notes. Colour saved before then stays in the data (the editor's schema keeps the attribute, so a later save doesn't drop it) but is drawn as normal text in the editor and in `RichText.vue` (`sanitiseDoc()` no longer passes it on; tested in `src/richText.test.ts`). Short fields are plain inputs.
  - **No separate registration form.** `my pilot` shows an empty modular pilot page when you have no pilot, and every section (identity included) is filled in place. The first save creates the pending pilot.
  - Sections that need LANCER data (equipment, talents, licences and so on) show with their `[ + ]` boxes marked "module offline" until the packs are imported. Mechs work straight away.
  - Pilot stats and mech skills get their own section on the page, added once their starting values are decided.
- **Approved pilots** get the same sheet read-only, with no note about it (the developer removed it, 2026-10-29).
- **Shared pieces:**
  - `src/myPilot.ts` loads and saves your own pilot (or, given an id, loads anyone's read-only for their record). Saves queue up and run in order, so autosaves can't overtake each other.
  - `src/gameData.ts` loads the pilot catalogues (pilot gear from `content_items`, bionics with their bonuses, pilot stats) and the shared item shape (`Item`, `toItem`). `src/mechData.ts` loads the mech catalogues (frames, weapons, systems from `content_items`).
  - `.sheet-label` (in `src/style.css`) is the section heading: 14px, bold, UPPERCASE and tracked, in `--text`, left-aligned. A GM-only or owner-only tag goes after the title as `<span class="private">GM only</span>`, which draws the lock (there is no "Private" tag; `StatRow`, `SheetField` and `LoadoutBox` have no `tag` prop). In a flex header it takes the free space, so extra info sits at the right; a header row with a label plus extra info gets `.sheet-head`, which moves the gap below onto the row.
  - `src/components/RichTextEditor.vue` and `RichText.vue`. Anything showing nothing uses the one `.empty` state (a dashed box, a centred sentence, an optional button); `AddBox.vue` and the `// empty //` / "none." variants are gone. (The older panel components, the placeholder page and the glitching titles were removed in the scope cut.)
  - `src/richTextSafe.ts` (the allowlist sanitiser), `loadoutSlots()` and the mech rules are tested in `src/*.test.ts` (`npm test`).
