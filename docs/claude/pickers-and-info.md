# Pickers and info

Covers: Pickers, the full information dialog and frame pickers.
See also: [item-cards.md](item-cards.md), [pilot-inventory.md](pilot-inventory.md).

- **Dialogs** (2026-11-15): pickers (`ItemPicker.vue`, `LoadoutBox.vue`'s), the full information and the "are you sure?" share one frame, `.dialog` in `src/style.css`: panel background, an `--mf` border (3px on top), a `.dialog-head` row (title, then `Close` / `Done`), an optional `.dialog-bar` and a `.dialog-body` that alone scrolls, so the dialog is never taller than the screen. **On phones** (under 40rem, 2026-11-20) every `dialog.dialog` is a full-screen sheet (the head stays at the top with `Close`, only the body scrolls, clear of the home bar) except `.compact` ones, the confirm and medal boxes, which stay centred; the page behind is locked while a dialog is open (`html:has(dialog:modal)`). The search field no longer autofocuses on touch screens (`ItemPicker.vue`, `LoadoutBox.vue`), picker row names get a 44px tap area on phones, and frame cards on phones show their stats before the radar. The frame information's radar uses `RadarChart`'s `wrap` prop on phones (each word of the name on its own line, the number with the last; labels about 12px, with room at the sides inside the chart's box). Pickers put a labelled search field in the bar (focused on open) with a match count ("12 of 40"), and a `.empty` "Nothing matches that search." **Picker rows are visual, not wordy** (the developer, 2026-11-19): `PickList.vue` draws every option (`ItemPicker.vue` and `LoadoutBox.vue`'s biomod picker share it; options are `PickOption` in `src/pickerRows.ts`). A row: the kind's symbol on its `--type-*` colour (a tiny `RadarChart` `mini` for frames and licences), the name (an `InfoButton`: the long text lives in the full information), a strip of symbols with mono numbers (`ItemStats` `compact`: the word is dropped when the symbol says it; every symbol keeps its name for screen readers and a hover title such as "Evasion 8"), a few chips, and the small `Choose` / `Add` button at the right. The rules text is never shown, only searched (`search`; the search also covers the name, chips, stat words and box title). What each kind shows (`itemPick()`, `framePick()`, `bonusStats()` in `pickerRows.ts`): **weapons** licence rank chip, mount and type chip, damage then range with their symbols (flat damage, damage colours), SP, at most two key tags (`KEY_TAGS`: Unique, AI, Limited, Loading, AP...); **systems** rank, type if not plain "System", SP, up to three key tags; **mods** rank, what they fit (up to three weapon types, "Any weapon"), SP; **frames** (frame picker) size, HP, evasion, e-defence, speed, armour, heat cap and SP with their symbols, a mini radar of six stats, rank chip, role chips, mount chips; **licences** the same without the rank chip (a licence is bought at rank I), named without the maker's prefix; **talents** the talent symbol, a "3 ranks" chip and the one-line `terse` summary clipped to a line; **core bonuses** the lime core symbol, any permanent stat numbers it adds,; what a bonus needs is only the blocked reason beside the button; **pilot gear** armour's bonuses (HP, armour, e-defence, speed), a weapon's range and damage, gear's key tags; **biomods** the slot chip, each stat bonus with that stat's own symbol (+1 beside the dumbbell), mod points ("2 mod points", as on the card) and manna price. **Boxes by manufacturer** (`groupPicks()`, `makerGroup()`; frames, licences, weapons, systems, mods, core bonuses, biomods; pilot gear, talents and the lore pickers are one plain list): a box per manufacturer like the archives' list (logo, id and name in the header, the colour only on the logo and a 3px top border, never text or a tint; "Other" and makers with no colour get the neutral rule colour, since lilac means clickable). Inside a box the options that can be chosen come first; boxes with nothing choosable (HORUS and NHC for players) go after the others, otherwise manufacturers keep their order; **Exotic is always last**: the Exotic box (`makerGroup()` knows the constant `EXOTIC_MAKER`: title "Exotic", neutral rule colour, no logo) after "Other", and Exotic-tagged items (`PickOption.exotic`) last inside whatever box they're in (chosen so players see what they can take before what they can't, without losing the grouping). On phones (under 40rem) a blocked row gives its reason a line of its own under the symbols, so they keep the full width; a blocked option is dimmed, its button disabled with the reason beside it (`aria-describedby`).
- **Frame pickers show the frame's art** (2026-11-19): a frame's row (`PickList.vue`, `framePick()` in `pickerRows.ts`, which now also takes the frames for the alternate fallback) has a 64px picture (48px on phones, whole, never cropped) where the mini radar was, and the radar only when a frame has no picture. A picture reads a mech at a glance, while the stat strip on the row already carries the numbers the radar hinted at; the full radar is in the frame's information. The frame's full information and the archives' frame and licence panes show the art first (`Info.art`, `InfoBody.vue`: centred, a `--raised` bordered frame, at most 18rem tall).
- **Full information** (2026-10-31; left-aligned in the redesign): every name of something a pilot can buy or equip opens a dialog with everything about it (the one exception, 2026-11-18: the licence's own information, in the dialog and the archives pane, links each rank's unlocks to their archives entry, `InfoButton`'s `to` with `archiveLink()` in `info.ts`; `UnlockList.vue` draws them with `links` there, and with `cards` as bars like an item card's header (the `--type-*` colour, white text, the type's symbol; mods in the system colour; 2026-11-21), and on the pilot sheet's ladder, where nobody should be navigated away, the same list opens the dialog): item cards, licence ranks and their unlocks, talents, core bonuses, bionics, the frame (`frame details` under the frame button, or the frame name when read-only), and every picker option, so players can read an option in full before choosing it (the dialog opens on top of the picker; Escape closes just it). `InfoDialog.vue`, mounted once in `App.vue`: the head (the maker's logo if any, the kind as a `.label`, the name at 20px, `Close`), then the facts as a `.sep` line, the numbers (a frame's as a neutral radar chart like COMP/CON's, `RadarChart.vue`, the same axes as the mech page's radar (`radarAxes()` in `src/mechOverview.ts`, carried as `Info.radar`; 2026-11-20): every stat that differs between frames, size included, in the stat tiles' order, placed between the lowest and highest any frame has; each label is the full stat name and number with the stat's symbol between it and the chart (beside it at the sides, above or below at the top and bottom, in the label colour; the pickers' mini radar has none); with all twelve numbers beside it in a 2 × 6 grid, stacked on phones; anything else's with `ItemStats.vue`, and "Costs 2 system points."), the lore (small, dim, italic; frame and core system lore keep spaced paragraphs, item descriptions run together), then groups under a `.label` title: rules and actions on the dialog's surface, each under a thin rule (`InfoRule.vue`: an action's `ActionChip`, name and frequency, its range and damage, its rules), stacked in reading order for an item's rules (`stack`), side by side otherwise; tags (`tags` groups) as chips each followed by what it means. A frame's groups are item cards (`look` on the group): each **trait** an `ItemCard` in the frame colour with the trait symbol and name, then its rules (chip first) and its other actions (`InfoCard.actions`), in a grid of equal-height cards; the **core system** one card in the core colour (dark text) titled with its name, holding its lore, passive and power (chip, numbers, Trigger/Effect). The body (everything under the head) is `InfoBody.vue`, shared with the archives' detail pane. The manufacturer's colour is only the dialog's border (`--mf`, through `makerColour()`; manufacturers are fetched the first time one is needed). `src/info.ts` builds the contents (`itemInfo`, `frameInfo(frame, allFrames)`, `talentInfo`, `coreBonusInfo`, `bionicInfo`); its `TONES` remain in the data but aren't drawn any more. `InfoButton.vue` is the clickable name: a real button in its surroundings' colour that underlines on hover and focus. Tag descriptions (`tagDescriptions`) and licence names (`licenceNames`) are shared lookups filled when the catalogues load. It ends with the same "Source:" line (`Info.id`, set by the pack-content builders). Tested in `src/info.test.ts`.
