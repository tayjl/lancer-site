# Mech page

Covers: Mechs: the hangar, the mech page, frame, stats, resources and effects.
See also: [mech-loadout.md](mech-loadout.md), [stats.md](stats.md), [images.md](images.md).

- **Mechs:** a pilot can have **several**. Each mech has:
  - a frame
  - a name, appearance description and image (all public)
  - a **manufacturer ID** the player makes up, in the format `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` (assumed: letters and digits). It's **public**, and should be unique. Uniqueness isn't critical, so the site checks for duplicates and warns, rather than the database enforcing it.
  - weapons on the **mounts its frame provides** (for example, Blackbeard has Flex, Main and Heavy)
  - systems, shown as system points used against the total. System points are calculated, not stored.
- **Mech hangar** (`MechHangar.vue`, 07 Hangar, its count and `New mech` in the section head): `New mech` (a small button with a plus, `New mech · 500` from the second mech, blocked with "Not enough manna" when the balance can't cover it) in the heading while editable, the cards (15rem each) centred as a group, and the empty state ("No mechs yet." / "No mechs.") when there are none. A card is a showcase, so centred: a `MechImage` (size `card`, 8rem tall: the mech's own image, else its frame's art, else the maker's logo, else a frame symbol; see [images.md](images.md)), the mech's name in `--text`, the frame, the class (dim), the manufacturer id in mono. The maker shows as a 3px top border (`--mf`), and as the logo only in the no-art placeholder. Every viewer sees the frame now.
- **Mechs are separate objects** with their own page at `/pilot/mechs/:mechId` (`src/views/MechView.vue`), laid out like my pilot: a breadcrumb (`My pilot / <mech>` back to the mechs tab, or `<list> / <CALLSIGN> / <mech>` on a record), then the **registration plate** (the prototype's): the maker's logo (56px; its code as a stamp in `--text` without a bundled logo; a frame symbol with no frame), the mech's name (28px, `--text`; an inline field on your own), a `.sep` line (the manufacturer id in mono, the frame button and "Frame details" while editable, the class), the save status under it, then Pilot, Size and System points (used / total, red only when over) as labels over values. Phones: a 40px logo and the figures on a row under a rule. On your own mech while a change is open, a `--void` bar joined to the plate's foot with the Draft / Submitted badge and where to submit or edit it. Then its rule check, then the slim sticky tabs, remembered as `?tab=`: **profile** (for a mech with a frame, first the "Overview" panel (`MechOverview.vue`, `src/mechOverview.ts`, tested in `src/mechOverview.test.ts`), then appearance, history, an "Image" panel, only while editable, holding just the upload controls and a one-line caption ("Showing your image." / "Showing the frame image. Upload your own to replace it." / "No image yet."; the picture itself is in the overview), and for an editable mech a "Danger zone" with `Scrap mech`; one column under 56rem). **Overview** (2026-11-19, symbols and numbers rather than text; the plate above already names the frame and maker, so the panel doesn't): a top row of two even columns, centred as a showcase: the frame's art (a `MechImage` of size `overview`, 16rem tall, 12rem on phones) with the role, size and licence-rank chips under it and the maker's logo small (28px, in the maker's colour) as the first chip, or, for a frame with no art, the maker's logo as the dim placeholder, and beside it a radar of this build's own HP, heat, evasion, speed, e-defence, tech attack and system points with their numbers in the labels, placed between the lowest and highest any frame has and clamped to the rim (`RadarChart` `tight`, `labelSize`, up to 28rem); one column on phones. The frame information's radar wraps its labels on phones (`wrap`, see [pickers-and-info.md](pickers-and-info.md)). Then the system points meter; the loadout as a diagram, a box per mount (Flex, Main, Heavy...) whose weapon places sit on the mount's own surface parted by thin `--rule` lines (no inset boxes): a weapon-type symbol on the weapon grey, its name, damage then range as the card shows them, modifiers applied, any mod as a chip; an empty place is one slim dashed line saying "Empty"; an "Integrated" box for weapons built into the frame, and the installed systems as green-tiled chips with their SP; then bars for "Damage" (all equipped weapons added up, flat numbers per damage type, in the damage colours) and the reach (shortest to longest of each range type, with its symbol). A mech with no frame has none of it. **stats** (mech stats, the resources tiles, "other effects and conditions") and **loadout** (the system points meter, core bonus assignments, weapon mounts and systems), for every viewer. Public mech fields live in `characters.mechs`; history, frame, mounts, systems (and quirks, off the page for now like traits) in `sheet.mechs[mechId]` (`MechPrivate` in `src/myPilot.ts`).
  - **Manufacturer colour** (2026-11-16): `--mf` from the frame's `source`, matched against the manufacturers in the packs (`useManufacturers()`, `makerColour()`, `makerLogo()` in `src/archive.ts`), so a future LCP's manufacturer works with no code. It is only the logo and the plate's 3px top border: no tint, headings, labels, item names or tiles in it. Frames are public now, so every viewer's plate and hangar cards show the maker. LCP `logo_url` images aren't loaded (they'd make every viewer contact an outside site).
  - **Resources** (stats tab): AI capacity, Limited uses, structure repair cost and overcharge heat as tiles like the stat tiles (mono value, uppercase label), then any permanent check accuracy lines.
  - **Other effects and conditions** (stats tab): the frame's traits as cards like the information dialog's (frame colour, trait symbol and name; the trait's rules, its other actions and uncounted bonuses inside, grouped by the reminder's `from`), in a grid of equal-height cards; then the core system as one core-coloured card (its lore, "Passive" and "Core power" each with chip, name and text); then everything else (core bonuses, talent ranks, equipment, missing content) as the prototype's `.effects` rows, parted by thin rules: its symbol, an uppercase kind label ("Core bonus", "Talent: Brawler I"), the action chip if any and the bold name, the rules text, and a dim "Not counted in the stats above." for uncounted bonuses.
  - **The frame** is picked on the plate (an `IPS-N Blackbeard` button while editable, a plain info button for everyone else). Licensed frames need rank II in their licence; GMS frames (e.g. the Everest) need none. **GM-only content** (the developer, 2026-11-18, reworked 2026-11-19): two rules, in `src/rules.ts`, tested in `src/rules.test.ts`. **HORUS and NHC** (`gmOnly()`, `GM_ONLY_NAMES`; a source, type, `license` or category of that name, or a licence whose frame is theirs, ignoring case) are blocked for players in every picker (frames, weapons, systems, mods, pilot gear, licences, core bonuses, talents, biomods) with the blocked pattern and "GM only"; GMs and owners pick them anywhere (a licence a pilot already holds, granted by a GM, can still be raised with manna). **Exotic** (`isExotic()`) is selectable **only in a GM's edit mode** (`gmEditing`), blocked everywhere else with "GM edit only", also for GMs and owners on their own pilots outside edit mode. An item is Exotic if its `source` is `EXOTIC` (the pseudo-manufacturer, any case: owner-made items with no mainline manufacturer) or it carries the Exotic Gear tag `tg_exotic` (one-of-a-kind prototypes that may have a real manufacturer), on the item or in its raw data, tags as objects or plain ids (the exact id, so `tg_exoskeleton` doesn't match). Biomods count by `source` only. Type, category, licence and pack names no longer count. Every picker asks `useRestriction()` (`src/restriction.ts`): the pilot and mech pages `provide(GM_EDIT, gmEditing)` and each picker reads it, so there is no per-picker prop; `restrictionOf(thing)` gives `{ gmOnly, exotic }` and `restrictionReason()` the sentence. Enforced in the pickers, and for players also by the database at submit ([security.md](security.md), Security rules); GM review is the backstop. Items a GM added aren't flagged. The picker lists the unlocked ones first and blocks the rest ("needs licence II"). Changing it takes the weapons off the mounts (after an "are you sure?"), since the new frame's mounts differ. It isn't in the loadout (the developer's choice, 2026-10-29).
  - **Mech stats** are a `StatRow` with LANCER's symbols, worked out by `mechStats()` in `src/mechData.ts` from the frame, HASE and supported equipment/talent/core bonuses (see Stat modifiers, [stats.md](stats.md)). Unset HASE counts as 0; hp = frame + 2×hull, repair cap = frame + floor(hull÷2), heat cap = frame + engineering, evasion = frame + agility, speed = frame + floor(agility/2), e-defence and tech attack = frame + systems, system points = frame + floor(systems÷2), no grit. "–" until a frame is chosen. Stat sources and other-effect reminders are visible to everyone, like the loadout.
