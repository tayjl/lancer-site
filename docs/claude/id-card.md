# Id card

Covers: The Union pilot ID card, its PNG download and Discord link previews.
See also: [pilot-profile.md](pilot-profile.md), [service-record.md](service-record.md), [history.md](history.md).

- **Pilot ID card** (`PilotIdCard.vue` on the shared `IdCardFrame.vue`, `src/idCard.ts`, `src/idCardExport.ts`; its own section, 02 Pilot ID, centred at most 36rem wide, with `Copy link` and `Download card` in the section head; `Copy link`, approved or dead pilots only, copies the `/registry/<id>` address, the one with the Discord preview, since My pilot's `/pilot/...` address shows whoever opens it their own pilot; the mech card's copies `/registry/<id>/mechs/<mech>`, as does a `Copy link` on the mech page's plate, same rule; all three copy buttons, lore's too, share `useCopyLink()` in `src/copyLink.ts`): an in-universe Union pilot licence, a chip-card terminal read-out about the size of a credit card (an SVG drawn at 856 x 540, scaled to fit, 309px wide at 375) with the top-right and bottom-left corners cut off (`CUT`, 48). The look (redesigned 2026-11-19 as "techy", not a flat slab): the dark panel with a faint 32px grid and a static diagonal sheen in the pilot colour; the pilot colour as edge light only (the two cut corners with a blurred glow, corner brackets at the square corners and around the portrait, a short rule under the header, the licence symbol, the medals' hexagon outlines and icons); thin `--rule` hairlines; a header with the licence symbol, "PILOT LICENCE", "UNION · " then the serial in mono under it (`pilotSerial(id)`, `UNI-0042-XXXX-XXXX`: the padded id then two Crockford base-32 codes from an FNV hash of it, deterministic; moved from the footer 2026-11-20) (pilots are licensed by Union, not HORUS: no HORUS branding on the card, and no department name, the developer, 2026-11-20) and a drawn contact chip; the portrait in a sharp window (the pilot symbol when none) with scan lines and a tint toward the pilot colour; "CALLSIGN" as a small mono label over the callsign (52 units, weight 600, tidied 2026-11-20), the name under it; Level and Licence level as big mono figures, Status (with a lamp in its status colour: `--ok` approved, `--dead` dead, else `--wait`) and Issued (the approved date) smaller, every label in the same small uppercase mono (22 units); a footer with a deterministic barcode of the serial (`barcodeBars()` in `src/idCard.ts`: guard bars, bars from FNV hashes, tested) and, on the right, the commendation icons as hexagon medals (six at most, then "+N"). No microtext (it ran off the card). Everything stays in the one SVG, patterns, gradients, the blur filter and masks included, and they all survive the PNG. Colours are read from the site's tokens once, because a downloaded picture has no stylesheet. **`Download card`** makes a PNG with no library: the card's SVG is cloned, every `<image>` (portrait, licence symbol, medal icons) and the two latin-subset fonts are fetched and put in as data urls (an SVG drawn as an image can't load anything outside), then it is drawn to a 2x canvas (1712 x 1080) and saved as `<callsign>-id.png`. A picture that can't be fetched is left out and the note says so ("Downloaded, without the portrait (it couldn't be fetched)."). The card shows what the page shows, so while you have an open change on your own pilot it carries the change's draft figures and card.
  - **Link previews** (Discord, 2026-11-20): a small Cloudflare Worker (`worker/`) runs only for `/registry/<id>`: it returns `index.html` with og tags (title, description, colour) for approved or dead pilots, read through the public `pilot_card()` function. The card picture can't be drawn in the Worker (free-plan CPU limit), so the pilot's own browser saves it: `PilotIdCard.vue`, silently, 2s after the card settles, when the pilot is approved or dead, no change is open, and the viewer is the pilot's player (living pilot) or a GM, draws the card at 856 x 540 (`cardToPng(svg, 1)`), uploads it to `character-images/<player_id>/card-<id>.png` (under 1 MB) and saves a fingerprint of what the card shows (`cardFingerprint()`) as `pilot_profiles.card_version`. The Worker puts `?v=<card_version>` on the image url so Discord fetches a fresh picture after any change. No saved picture yet: title and description only, no image tags.
  - **In the pilot lists** (2026-11-21): the registry, pending and memorial show every pilot's card ([registry.md](registry.md)). `extra` is optional on `PilotIdCard` / `IdCardFrame`: none means no `Download card` (the record page passes `pilot-id-extra`). Every SVG id starts with `id-card-<pilot id>` (the frame's `prefix` and the card's own clips and masks), so many cards on one page don't borrow each other's colours and clips.
- **Mech card** (2026-11-21; `MechIdCard.vue`, the mech page's last section "Registration card", see [mech-page.md](mech-page.md)): the pilot card's twin. Both draw on `IdCardFrame.vue` (the 856 x 540 SVG with the cut corners, grid, sheen, edge light, outline, scan and tint patterns, `Download card` teleported to the section head, and the silent save), so they look and save alike. The maker's colour (`makerColour`, only when the maker has one of its own) takes the pilot colour's place (edge light, cut corners, brackets, short header rule); without one the card is neutral (`--edge`). Header: the maker's logo (the frame symbol without one), the mech's name big in uppercase, then "SOURCE NAME · " and the manufacturer id in mono ("No frame" with none). Body: the mech's picture in a bracketed window on the left (its upload, cropped to fill; else the frame art, whole; else the maker's logo dim), with the pilot card's scan lines and tint; on the right the mech page's radar (`radarAxes()`), drawn by `RadarChart` in its `ink` mode (colours and font as attributes, each symbol a masked picture, labels just symbol and number, so it survives the PNG), or nothing without a frame (the header says "No frame"). Footer: "PILOT" over the callsign, Size and System points (used / total) as mono figures, and a barcode of the manufacturer id. Downloaded as `<mech name>-card.png`. Its picture is saved by the same rules as the pilot card's (the pilot's player while alive, or a GM; approved or dead; never from an open change on your own pilot; only once the packs have loaded) to `character-images/<player_id>/mech-card-<character id>-<mech id>.png`, and its fingerprint is merged into `pilot_profiles.mech_card_versions[<mech id>]` (through `usePilotProfile`, so other mechs' keys stay).
  - **Mech link previews:** the Worker also handles `/registry/<id>/mechs/<mech id>` (the record mech route), read through the public `mech_card()` function: og:title the mech's name, og:description "IPS-N Blackbeard. Pilot: WARDEN." (just "Pilot: …" with no frame), every player-typed value escaped, and the image only when `card_image` is the path `mech_card()` builds (checked again by pattern; the mech's own image field is never used). No theme colour: the maker's colour isn't readable without logging in. Tests: `worker/meta.test.ts`.
