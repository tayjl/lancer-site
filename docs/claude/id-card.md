# Id card

Covers: The Union pilot ID card, its PNG download and Discord link previews.
See also: [pilot-profile.md](pilot-profile.md), [service-record.md](service-record.md), [history.md](history.md).

- **Pilot ID card** (`PilotIdCard.vue`, `src/idCard.ts`, `src/idCardExport.ts`; its own section, 02 Pilot ID, centred at most 36rem wide, with `Download card` in the section head): an in-universe Union pilot licence, a chip-card terminal read-out about the size of a credit card (an SVG drawn at 856 x 540, scaled to fit, 309px wide at 375) with the top-right and bottom-left corners cut off (`CUT`, 48). The look (redesigned 2026-11-19 as "techy", not a flat slab): the dark panel with a faint 32px grid and a static diagonal sheen in the pilot colour; the pilot colour as edge light only (the two cut corners with a blurred glow, corner brackets at the square corners and around the portrait, a short rule under the header, the licence symbol, the medals' hexagon outlines and icons); thin `--rule` hairlines; a header with the licence symbol, "PILOT LICENCE", "UNION · " then the serial in mono under it (`pilotSerial(id)`, `UNI-0042-XXXX-XXXX`: the padded id then two Crockford base-32 codes from an FNV hash of it, deterministic; moved from the footer 2026-11-20) (pilots are licensed by Union, not HORUS: no HORUS branding on the card, and no department name, the developer, 2026-11-20) and a drawn contact chip; the portrait in a sharp window (the pilot symbol when none) with scan lines and a tint toward the pilot colour; "CALLSIGN" as a small mono label over the callsign (52 units, weight 600, tidied 2026-11-20), the name under it; Level and Licence level as big mono figures, Status (with a lamp in its status colour: `--ok` approved, `--dead` dead, else `--wait`) and Issued (the approved date) smaller, every label in the same small uppercase mono (22 units); a footer with a deterministic barcode of the serial (`barcodeBars()` in `src/idCard.ts`: guard bars, bars from FNV hashes, tested) and, on the right, the commendation icons as hexagon medals (six at most, then "+N"). No microtext (it ran off the card). Everything stays in the one SVG, patterns, gradients, the blur filter and masks included, and they all survive the PNG. Colours are read from the site's tokens once, because a downloaded picture has no stylesheet. **`Download card`** makes a PNG with no library: the card's SVG is cloned, every `<image>` (portrait, licence symbol, medal icons) and the two latin-subset fonts are fetched and put in as data urls (an SVG drawn as an image can't load anything outside), then it is drawn to a 2x canvas (1712 x 1080) and saved as `<callsign>-id.png`. A picture that can't be fetched is left out and the note says so ("Downloaded, without the portrait (it couldn't be fetched)."). The card shows what the page shows, so while you have an open change on your own pilot it carries the change's draft figures and card.
  - **Link previews** (Discord, 2026-11-20): a small Cloudflare Worker (`worker/`) runs only for `/registry/<id>`: it returns `index.html` with og tags (title, description, colour) for approved or dead pilots, read through the public `pilot_card()` function. The card picture can't be drawn in the Worker (free-plan CPU limit), so the pilot's own browser saves it: `PilotIdCard.vue`, silently, 2s after the card settles, when the pilot is approved or dead, no change is open, and the viewer is the pilot's player (living pilot) or a GM, draws the card at 856 x 540 (`cardToPng(svg, 1)`), uploads it to `character-images/<player_id>/card-<id>.png` (under 1 MB) and saves a fingerprint of what the card shows (`cardFingerprint()`) as `pilot_profiles.card_version`. The Worker puts `?v=<card_version>` on the image url so Discord fetches a fresh picture after any change. No saved picture yet: title and description only, no image tags.
