# Pilot sheet, mech page, item cards, pickers

Purpose: the layout and behaviour of the my pilot sheet (cover, one-file sections, stats, licences, loadout), item cards, pickers, the full information dialog and the mech page. Read when a task touches PilotManageView, MechView or their components.

## my pilot and mechs (moved from Current status and next step)

- **my pilot is built** as a COMP/CON-style sheet in the centred page column (`src/views/PilotManageView.vue`; redesigned 2026-11-15):
  - **Breadcrumb** on records and GM review (`registry / WARDEN`, `pending /`, `memorial /` by status, or `gm: pilots /`); no big page title, the callsign is the title. The global `.crumbs` style (`style.css`) is shared with `PageHeader.vue`.
  - **Cover** (2026-11-20, replacing the old header; `PilotCover.vue`, mockup `docs/redesign/dossier/`): a banner strip (the picture, else a plain gradient from the pilot colour into `--void` with one soft glow, no stripes; the cover body is a flow-root so its faint grid starts under the banner, never across it), the portrait in pilot-colour corner brackets, a small mono classification line ("Union Administrative Department · Pilot file · `pilotSerial(id)`", phones drop the department) with the pilot's status `.badge` at its right end (before any GM links, alone in the corner without them; it wraps to its own line on phones, still visible), the callsign big with the name beside it on the same baseline (24px `--text-dim`, 20px / 18px on phones, wrapping under the callsign when there isn't room; both inline fields where editable; the name isn't repeated when there's no callsign), the motto (20px italic in pilot-colour quotation marks drawn by CSS, never stored; the editable textarea has no underline, only an outline on hover and focus), then a foot row with the medals (`CommendationBadges.vue`) and "Player: x" small and dim at its right end, under the actions (its own left-aligned line on phones), then the figures strip: Level, Licence level, Grit, HP and, only for the pilot's player and GMs, Manna left. While editing, the cover's `tools` slot holds the Portrait, Banner (`ImageUpload`, plus `Position banner`) and Colour (`PilotColourField`, `Reset colour` on the same line as the boxes) controls along its foot, three equal top-aligned columns under one rule (stacked on phones). **Banner positioning** (`Position banner`, and straight after a new banner saves): the strip comes in front of the portrait and is dragged (pointer events, so mouse and touch; `touch-action: none` while positioning) or nudged with the arrow keys when focused (Shift for bigger steps, Escape cancels); a panel over its foot holds a `Zoom` slider (1 to 3), `Save position` and `Cancel`. The position is `object-position` in percent plus `scale(zoom)` from the same point, so it covers the strip at any width. The faint grid and the corner brackets in `--pilot` are decoration. GMs get `GM review` / `GM controls` and `Edit pilot` at the right of the classification line, after the status badge.
  - **"What happens next" bar**: a `--void` band joined to the header's foot, on your own sheet and any dead pilot's: a sentence-case sentence (a badge only for a change, since the cover already shows the pilot's own status), the save status ("Saving…", "Saved", red "Not saved: …"; `useSaveStatus()` in `src/saveStatus.ts`, drawn by `SaveStatus.vue`, which the mech page, GM edit bar and lore editor use too), then `.small` buttons at the right (secondary first, at most one `.primary`): a new pilot as a draft ("Draft": "GMs see it once you submit it.", `Submit pilot`), submitted ("Pending": "Waiting on a GM.", read-only, `Edit pilot` takes it back to a draft, no confirm), sent back ("Sent back", the GM's note, editable, `Resubmit pilot`), approved (`Propose changes`), drafting ("Draft": `Withdraw change`, `Submit change`), a change a GM sent back ("Sent back", the GM's note, editable, `Withdraw change`, `Submit change`), submitted ("Submitted": `Edit change`, `Withdraw change`), dead (read-only). On phones the sentence takes a row and the buttons share the next. Long notes sit in a `.notice` under it with a `.notice-title` ("Note from the GM", with the quiet `Clear note` once approved; also for a sent-back change), then the rule check.
  - **One scrolling file, no tabs** (2026-11-20): numbered sections (numbers come from position among the visible ones), `#identity`, `#pilot-id`, `#service-record`, `#capabilities`, `#qualifications`, `#equipment`, `#hangar`, `#gm-controls` (GMs on the review route only), `#gallery` (`FileSection.vue`: mono number, title, a short pilot-colour dash on a hairline, extras at the right; on phones under 40rem the hairline hides and the extras sit at the right of the title, wrapping as one group; a section's own component teleports its buttons and count into `#<id>-extra`, as `PilotIdCard`, `ServiceRecord`, `MechHangar` and `PilotGallery` do). `FileIndex.vue`: a sticky list beside the file on wide screens, under 56rem one sticky line under the top bar showing the current section, which opens the same list (2026-11-20); an `IntersectionObserver` marks the section in view (`aria-current`), a click scrolls and `router.replace`s the `#hash`, so `/pilot#hangar` links and reloads land there (the mech page's back link is `#hangar`; the mech page keeps its own `?tab=` tabs). **Every section after Identity folds** (2026-11-20): the title is a button (`aria-expanded`, chevron before the number, lilac only on hover/focus), the body stays mounted with `v-show` so unsaved fields and open pickers survive, head extras stay visible; Identity is `fixed`. Open by default; each section's state is kept per viewer in localStorage (`src/fileSections.ts`, works without storage), and an index click or a `#section` link opens the section before scrolling. Every section shows for every viewer, except the gallery, which read-only viewers don't get when it's empty. Sections sit on the page rather than in `.panel`s; boxes stay for item cards, the ID card and the danger zone. GM controls (`GmReview`: the pending Decision form, or Rewards, reputation and the danger zone) is its own section on the review route (2026-11-20).
  - **01 Identity** (`IdentityGrid.vue` at full width: form-like cells for age, gender, sexuality, height, weight, eyes and hair, the last one widening so no cell is left empty; fields while editable), then Appearance, Personality and Backstory, one under another. The writing headings (`SheetField.vue`) are 16px, bold and tracked with a thin rule under the row, so they don't read as a log entry's label. **02 Pilot ID**: `PilotIdCard` alone, centred as a showcase (at most 36rem), its `Download card` in the section head. **03 Service record**: the timeline, full width. Your own approved pilot gets a "Danger zone" panel at the very end of the file (`Mark pilot dead`).
  - **Always editable, no edit buttons** (while pending): appearance, personality and backstory are open editors that save themselves 0.8s after you stop typing (`src/components/SheetField.vue`; an empty editor shows a quiet "write here..."). Callsign and name are inline inputs, the table normal inputs, all saved when you leave them. Traits are off the sheet for now (the `traits` column and `src/entries.ts`, the list parser, are kept; nothing shows them).
  - **Below, full width:**
    - **04 Capabilities:** `StatFigures.vue` (general stats as a row of five filled tiles), then Personal stats and Piloting stats (HASE) as full-width `StatRow.vue` tile rows, one above the other, set apart by space and their headings (`.stat-block`, no hairlines), each block (general, personal, piloting) followed by its own "Where these numbers come from" (`StatSources.vue`, which `StatRow` renders itself; hidden when nothing modifies that block). Every tile (general, personal, HASE, mech stats and mech resources) is filled one step lighter than what it sits on (`--panel` on the page, `--raised` on a panel, set through `--tile`), with no outline and a 3px strip of the pilot's colour along its top (`--pilot`, `--edge` without one; decoration only); numbers 30px.
    - **Personal stats** (from the `pilot_stats` table) and **piloting stats** (HASE): `src/components/StatRow.vue` on the pilot file and the mech page: the section heading with, for budgets, pips (one per point, filled while still to spend, hidden over 12 points, decoration only) and "1 of 4 points left" at the right (numbers in mono; red "1 over the 4 points" only when overspent), then a tile per stat: its symbol (personal stats and HASE have their own, `STAT_ICONS` in `src/gameData.ts`; size's shows the size), the number in mono in `--text` (the effective total), the uppercase name and - / + pinned to the tile's foot, so they line up across a row (a disabled one says why on hover). A modified personal stat's breakdown ("Base: 2", "Arm: +1") is in the block's "Where these numbers come from" (`sheetStats()` sets `contributors` only when something changes the stat). Phones (under 40rem): budgeted blocks (personal stats, HASE) become one filled row per stat with a small gap between rows and the colour strip down the left (symbol, name, number, - / +); general and mech stats stay small tiles, at least 76px wide (four to a row at 390px, three on the narrowest phones), with a two-word name wrapping onto a second centred line; words never break. Each block's sources are listed under it in "Where these numbers come from". Both are visible to everyone logged in. Personal bases start at 2, stay within 2–4, and share 4 extra allocation points. HASE remains 0–6, starts at 0 and shares 2 + level points. Each + stops at its base maximum or when its own budget is spent; lowering refunds points. Biomod bonuses do not use allocation points or alter the base limits; the tile shows the effective total and the sources list the base and each modifier, including negative ones. Personal rule checks also run while game content is unavailable. Stored in `sheet.pilot_stats` and `sheet.mech_skills`.
    - **Kept in mind for later (the developer's option A):** summary tiles, each group a bordered tile with its title and a one-line summary (e.g. "hp 6 · hase 4/5", "level 3 · 1 core bonus") that opens the group.
    - **Licences**, **core bonuses** and **talents** (05 Qualifications, stacked full width: Licences, Talents, then Core bonuses as a grid of cards; the page uses `RankPanel`'s compact `rows` mode: a frame-art thumbnail with the maker's logo and code, the name opening its information, an "Unlocks…" line, rank pips I II III with held ones filled and the numeral always shown, `Lower rank` / `Raise rank · 600` on the row while editable and the alternate-frame radios under it; talents the same with a brown tile and their summary; the item-card look below stays in the component), shared by my pilot, pilot records and GM pilot reviews (visible to everyone logged in). Licences and talents are `src/components/RankPanel.vue`: an item card per one held (licences purple with the licence symbol, talents brown; the rank, "Rank II", at the header's right), and `Add licence · 600` / `Add talent · 300` in the heading opening a picker. Licences (`columns`) lay ranks I to III side by side like COMP/CON's ladder (one column on phones): each rank box has its numeral, then "✓ Held" / "Next: 600 manna" / "Locked" (prices only while editable), then its unlocks, each with its type symbol (frame, weapon, system) and opening its information. Talents (`tiers`) stack ranks I to III, parted by thin rules on the card's surface: a big numeral, the rank's name and its rules, left-aligned. Licence rank columns are thin outlines with no fill. Later ranks are dimmed. The card's foot has `Lower rank` (`Remove licence` at rank I) and `Raise rank · 600`, which uses the blocked pattern when the manna runs out (disabled, keeping its label, "Not enough manna" beside it). The licences heading shows the licence level. **Frame art** (2026-11-19): a licence card shows the art of the frame the licence gives (the pilot's choice among its alternates) beside the ladder, 11rem wide and whole (`.with-art` in `RankPanel.vue`, a `MechImage` of size `licence`), above it on phones (at most 8rem tall); a frame with no art shows the maker's logo as the placeholder. The licence picker rows and the frame picker show it too. Core bonuses are `src/components/CoreBonusPanel.vue`: "1 of 1 picks used" in the heading, a lime card (dark text) per bonus with its maker at the right, and, while editable, the empty state for the next pick ("1 pick left." with `Add core bonus`, or "Next pick at 6 licence ranks."). Stored in `sheet.licences` (licence id, which is the original frame's id, to rank), `sheet.frame_choices` (licence id to the frame it gives, alternates only), `sheet.talents` (talent id to rank) and `sheet.core_bonuses` (ids). Rank II of a licence unlocks the frame; each rank unlocks that frame's gear of that licence level. Talents and core bonuses come from lancer-data (`useGameData()`).
    - **Rule check** (`src/components/RuleCheck.vue`, a `.notice.warn` with a sentence-case title: "Fix 1 problem before you submit" on your own draft, "Fix 1 problem" on your own editable sheet or mech (`fix` prop), "1 problem" anywhere else): under the header, listing whatever breaks the rules (`pilotProblems()` in `src/rules.ts`), e.g. manna overspent or HASE over the level's points. Hidden when everything is legal. The mech page shows its own mech's problems, and the GM decision box shows them above approve (approving with problems asks first).
    - **Bionics**, then **loadout**, both visible to everyone logged in. Bionics are `src/components/LoadoutBox.vue`: "Mod points 1 / 9" and `Edit biomods` in the heading (a picker that adds and removes existing items only), then a pilot-coloured item card per bionic with its mod points at the right, its stat bonuses with their symbols and its description. Bionics count their mod-point cost against the **mod limit** (6 + level), the picker won't add one that goes over, and adding stops when the manna can't cover that biomod's price ("Not enough manna"). Stored as ids in `sheet.bionics`.
    - **The loadout** (`src/components/PilotLoadout.vue`) is six slots in one three-column grid (a plain grid, not `.slot-grid`; cards top-aligned, so a tall one never stretches the others): Armour, Weapon, Weapon on the first row, Gear, Gear, Gear on the second, each card under its own small uppercase type label (`SlotCard`'s `label`), so the type never depends on position; on phones (under 64rem) one column in the same order, with the biomods in their own row under the loadout. Empty slots show the empty state ("No gear yet." with `Add gear`); read-only sheets skip them, so the filled cards close up in order ("No pilot gear." when all are empty). **Inventory** (2026-11-21): pilot gear is owned once bought (`sheet.owned_gear`: `{ key, id, notes?, free? }` per copy, duplicates allowed; `free` is set by the database only) and equipped by key (`sheet.equipped_gear`); `equippedGear()` and `loadoutSlots()` in `src/gameData.ts` sort the equipped items into slots by `type`, each type in equipped order. While the pilot can equip, each card's foot has `Swap` and `Remove`. **Approved pilot** (own, or a GM in edit mode; even with a change open): a slot's picker offers its owned, unequipped items of that type (their description as the dim line); choosing equips and `Remove` unequips, at once and with no approval, through `equipGear()` in `src/myPilot.ts` (`equip_pilot_gear`; only keys of known Armor/Weapon/Gear items are sent); save status as usual. Under the slots (approved pilots only), one folding toggle `Stowed {n} · Misc {m}` (chevron, lilac only on hover and focus, closed by default; hidden when nothing is stowed, there are no misc items and the viewer can't add any). Opened: **Stowed** = owned items not equipped (equipped ones are only in the slots), as plain one-line text rows, no cards, grouped Armour / Weapons / Gear / Misc with the small uppercase label in a narrow left column (above the rows under 36rem); only groups with rows show, plus Misc for a GM in edit mode. A row: the item's symbol (`itemCard()`'s icon), its name, then dim on the same line `Bought in this change · {price} manna` or its description in quotes, cut off with an ellipsis; items of other types or in no loaded pack go in Gear, the latter as `Unknown item ({id})`. A row is a button (`aria-expanded`) that opens it in place, one at a time: the price line, its `Description` (`ItemNote`, editable in a change), `Info` (the full information) and the GM's `Remove`, with `Not removed: …` there. `Buy item` is in the Loadout heading row. An item bought in the open change has its slot-picker row blocked "Needs GM approval". **Misc items** (`sheet.misc_items`: `{ key, name, notes? }`, GM only, the database refuses player changes) are free-text: name and description, never equipped, no price; only a GM in edit mode gets `Add misc item` (a small text button at the end of the Misc group; an inline form: `Name` required, max 60; `Description` max 140; `Add item` / `Discard changes`) and `Remove` in the opened row (same confirm as owned items). `Buy item` (change or GM edit mode) opens every pilot gear item with its price (`gearPrice()`: the item's `manna_cost`, 100 when unset), blocked "Not enough manna" or by the GM-only/Exotic rules; choosing adds a new copy (`crypto.randomUUID()` key), unequipped. Players can't remove owned items; a GM in edit mode can (`Remove`, then "{Item} will be taken off this pilot. This can't be undone." / `Remove item`). **Pilot being created** (pending): a slot picks from the whole catalogue and adds and equips a new copy in one step, `Remove` takes it off the sheet, and each card says `Free` or its price (`freeGear(…, null)`: the first 1 armour, 2 weapons and 3 gear are free). Dead pilots: read-only. Unknown ids are kept and counted.
    - **Item cards** (the prototype's ItemCard; pilot and mech loadouts, bionics, licences, talents, core bonuses): `ItemCard.vue` is the one card: a header bar in its type's colour (`--type-*`: weapon grey, system green, frame/licence purple, pilot gear/bionic teal, talent brown, core bonus lime with dark text) with the type's symbol, the name (an `InfoButton`) and, at the right, system points, mod points or a rank (hidden when 0); then the body and a foot for its buttons. Integrated items get a light line under the header. `ItemDetails.vue` fills it for a game item (`itemCard()`, `itemFacts()` and `itemRules()` in `src/itemDetails.ts`, tested in `src/itemDetails.test.ts`): the facts line (`.sep`: licence rank or maker, mount and type: "IPS-N Blackbeard II · Main Melee"; none for pilot gear), its numbers as symbol + mono number + small word (`ItemStats.vue`; damage symbols in their `--dmg-*` colour), its actions and deployables always in full, each on the card's surface under a thin rule (COMP/CON's active effects only when they're the item's only rules: `cardRules()`; `ActionChip.vue` first, then the name and frequency, the action's own range and damage, its rules), its rules text, "Where these numbers come from" (modifiers applied by `equipment.ts`) and its tag chips. `BuildItem.vue` wraps it on mechs, with the profile/prototype choices at the top of the body; a weapon with Variable damage gets a Damage type select (`Choose…` until picked, a rule problem until then). **Description** (2026-11-20): each equipped item (pilot gear, mounted mech weapons, installed systems; not built-in items) can carry its player's description, max 140 characters (`ItemNote.vue`): an italic line at the top of the body, or a `Description` text field while editable, saved on change. It goes through approval like the rest of the sheet, and goes when its item is swapped or removed (`src/itemNotes.ts`); pilot gear's lives on the owned copy (`owned_gear[].notes`), so it follows the item when it's swapped. `ActionChip.vue` is the clipped chip for Quick, Full, Reaction, Protocol, Free, Overcharge, Quick Tech, Full Tech and Invade (`activationOf()`: `--act-*` colour and cci symbol; passive draws nothing). `SlotCard.vue` is one place in a loadout: its name as a small uppercase label above (none for systems), then the card, or the empty state with a sentence and a button. Cards sit in a `.slot-grid` (`src/style.css`: CSS columns at least 20rem wide, each filling from the top like masonry, so short cards leave no gaps; cards read down each column, 2026-11-19). `ItemPicker.vue` is the searchable picker dialog. Cards for pack content (not bionics) end with a small dim "Source: <pack name>" line (`source` prop, `.pack-source` in `style.css`); `sourceOf(id)` in `gameData.ts` looks the pack up from one shared read of `content_items`, refreshed after `invalidateCatalogues()`.
    - **Dialogs** (2026-11-15): pickers (`ItemPicker.vue`, `LoadoutBox.vue`'s), the full information and the "are you sure?" share one frame, `.dialog` in `src/style.css`: panel background, an `--mf` border (3px on top), a `.dialog-head` row (title, then `Close` / `Done`), an optional `.dialog-bar` and a `.dialog-body` that alone scrolls, so the dialog is never taller than the screen.
