Appearance

Tall and narrow, with a shaved head and a burn scar that runs from her left ear to her collar. Always in a flight jacket two sizes too big, patched with the insignia of ships she has never served on.

Personality

Quiet until the shooting starts, then dry and unhurried on comms. Keeps promises to the letter and not one word further.

Backstory

Private

Grew up on a salvage tender in the Dawnline Shore. Learned to grapple wrecks before she learned to read, and joined a convoy escort after pirates took the tender. She has been chasing the same three ships ever since.

Nanocarbon Sword

IPS-NBlackbeard licence IIHeavy melee

IPS-N’s nanocarbon sword is a new spin on an old classic. Embedded nanosensors along the blade capture a full spectrum of data and transfer it to omninet storage banks for after-action review. Meanwhile, onboard software interprets the live feedback and adjusts the molecular composition of the blade edge in real time.
