Appearance

Tall and narrow-shouldered, with a pale scar running from her left ear to the collarbone. She wears her flight suit unzipped to the sternum and keeps her gloves on indoors.

A brass lantern pin on her collar is the only thing she polishes.

Personality

Patient to the point of stubbornness. Warden listens first, then says one thing and means it. She is dry, dependable and terrible at accepting thanks.

Good at holding a line, bad at leaving one.

Backstory
Only you and GMs can read this.

Isla grew up on a relay station that lost power for three winters. She learned to keep a light burning for the freighters that still came through, and stayed on after the others left.

The Union recruited her when she talked a drifting hauler down with a hand lamp and a borrowed radio. She has never told them how close it was.
